Method and sources

Duration. Each observation represents a hypothetical Treasury note with 10 years remaining and 20 semiannual payments. Its coupon rate equals that day’s 10-year constant-maturity yield, so it prices at par. Modified duration is the present-value-weighted average payment time divided by one plus the semiannual yield. It estimates the percentage price decline for a one-percentage-point increase in yield.

Modified duration formula.

Dmod = ∑ k=1 20 (k/2) PV(CFk) P 1+y/2

k is the six-month payment number, so k/2 is the payment time in years. PV(CFk) is the present value of that payment, discounted at the semiannual yield y/2. P is the sum of those present values, and y is the annual Treasury yield as a decimal.

Term premium and mortgages. The term premium is a New York Fed model estimate. Mortgage rates are Freddie Mac’s weekly national averages. The comparison measures changes between shared dates and describes how the two series moved over the window.
